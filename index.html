SMALL STEPS, BETTER HEALTH

Health & wellness notes.

Simple reminders to help you make informed everyday choices.

01 / PREVENTION

Make preventive check-ups a habit

Regular check-ups can help you discuss risk factors and identify concerns early. Ask your clinician which screenings suit you.

Speak to a doctor ↗
02 / EVERYDAY WELLNESS

Build movement into your day

Choose activities that suit your abilities and build up gradually. Consistency matters more than intensity.

Speak to a doctor ↗
03 / NUTRITION

Keep meals balanced

Include a variety of vegetables, fruits, whole grains and protein sources. Seek individual advice for specific needs.

Speak to a doctor ↗
